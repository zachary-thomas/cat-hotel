# Lower desks and a living neighborhood

Reception and milkshake worktops are 0.74 world units high, down from 1.22.
The cabinets, lettering, equipment and catalogue illustrations match the new
proportions. Staff stand on a small 0.18-unit step.

Each destination has six ambient voxel cats walking along sidewalks or a
promenade. Detailed homes, porches, mailboxes, gardens, lamps and trees surround
the buildable property. Their styles follow Meadow, Seaside, Forest and Snowcap.
Trees and buildings overlap in the view, with streets extending beyond camera
reach. All optional expansion parcels remain available for construction.

Manual panning and zooming stay near the hotel. Fit lot includes all expansion
parcels, and Focus selection still frames rooms or objects anywhere on the lot.
Sidewalk cats preserve their positions when the player edits the hotel, stay
separated, and freeze with the motion setting. They do not affect saved progress
or hotel income.

## Verification

All 17 creative suites pass. Native checks cover the five phone/desktop sizes,
100%/150% text, both counters, all four neighborhoods, camera limits and actual
voxel geometry along walking routes. Final review closed three issues: Snowcap
rocks obstructing a sidewalk, pines intersecting rotated houses, and close camera
panning into empty gaps between expansion parcels. Rear and side windows complete
the homes visible from the hotel. See [full verification](../verification.md).
