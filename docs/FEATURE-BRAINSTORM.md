# Purrington Hotel — Mobile Idle Feature Brainstorm

September 8, 2026 • Mobile-first, idle Cat Coins, and successive hotels

## Recommended concept

A chapter-based idle journey: earn Cat Coins, improve Meadow House, unlock Seaside Suites, and keep progressing through different hotels. Previously opened hotels keep earning. The emotional hook is returning to a tiny voxel world that becomes more beautiful with each upgrade.

## A world run by cats

Everyone in the everyday world is a cat: guests, hotel owners, receptionists, chefs, attendants, and neighbors. A bow-tied tuxedo cat welcomes travelers; a tabby concierge carries towels; a cat chef prepares room service. Staff accessories communicate jobs while ears, paws, tails, and feline faces keep every character recognizably a cat. Guests travel independently rather than being dropped off by humans.

Dogs, bunnies, and other animals may visit through optional later-game events. Possible ideas include a dog travel club booking a weekend or a bunny delegation visiting the garden. Cat staff host these visitors; the ordinary game remains cat-only. Event timing and rewards are undecided, and these species are outside the MVP.

## Three structures considered

| Structure | Strength | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sequential hotels with persistent income — recommended** | Clear chapters and lasting value from earlier work | Growing income needs careful balancing |
| One endlessly expanding hotel | Strong attachment to one place | Less variety than the requested hotel journey |
| Reset/prestige between hotels | Reuses early progression | Erasing work may feel punishing; defer |

## Destination ideas

**Meadow House:** sage cottage roof, sunlit garden, window perches, orange tabby mascot. A modest boarding house becomes a flourishing garden hotel.

**Seaside Suites:** blue roofs, shell cushions, rope scratching posts, shaded verandas. A small beach lodge grows into a breezy resort.

**Forest Lodge:** timber rooms, autumn leaves, log climbing furniture, panoramic forest windows. A woodland hideaway becomes an elaborate retreat.

**Snowcap Spa:** snowy roofs, knitted beds, amber windows, heated lounges. The cold exterior contrasts with cozy indoor spaces.

Later possibilities: cherry-blossom inn, city rooftop hotel, desert oasis, canal-side guesthouse. Finish and test the first two locations before extending the catalogue.

## Upgrades that feel rewarding

- Plain mat → pillowy bed → tiny four-poster cat bed.
- Single bowl → feeding station → cheerful room-service counter.
- Scratching post → climbing tree → voxel play castle.
- Reception stool → welcoming desk → grand cat-shaped reception.
- Bare window → sunny perch → cushioned observation nook.
- Exterior milestones add planters, signs, lights, and garden paths.

Use a few major visual tiers rather than bespoke artwork for every numeric level.

## Cat moments

Miso sleeps in the delivery box beside its new bed. Bean bats a fish-shaped mobile at Seaside Suites. Clover climbs Forest Lodge's tallest post. Mochi curls up beside a Snowcap Spa window.

These should occur automatically and be enjoyable without collecting a prize. Tapping can reveal a name or reaction.

## Meaningful decisions

- Buy a cheap income upgrade now or save for the next hotel.
- Improve a favorite zone or several efficient early upgrades.
- Finish renovating the current location or start the newly unlocked one.
- Pick a cosmetic theme after reaching a milestone.

Avoid hidden care penalties, staffing schedules, individual reservations, and mandatory tapping. Returning after an absence should feel welcoming.

## Sample return session

1. Collect 14,400 Cat Coins after two hours at 120/min.
2. Spend 480 on a suites upgrade and see its bed tier improve.
3. Notice progress toward the next hotel level.
4. Check the map's level and coin gates for Seaside Suites.
5. Discover a cat, then leave while owned hotels continue earning.

Numbers are illustrative; the full progression curve requires simulation and playtesting.

## Screenshot story

Main menu → active hotel → upgrade sheet → destination map → welcome-back reward. Five portrait screens communicate the mobile idle loop.

## MVP boundary

Two playable hotels, four upgrade zones per hotel, ten hotel levels, one coin wallet, online/offline income, automatic cats, a small collection, portrait touch menus, settings, and durable saving. Later hotel previews are clearly marked when not playable.
