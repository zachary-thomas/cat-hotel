# Mobile screenshot prompts

## Current worldbuilding constraint

All new generations and revisions must depict a world entirely inhabited and operated by cats. Staff, guests, residents, portraits, and character icons are feline. Working cats may wear small role accessories but retain cat faces, ears, paws, and tails; no humans or human-directed slogans. Dogs, bunnies, and other species are reserved for possible later-game events, not ordinary scenes or the MVP. The historical prompts below are retained verbatim for provenance; this constraint supersedes any ambiguous character or world wording in them.

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. These are concept mockups, not captures of a working game. The following are the exact final generation prompts, revised for mobile idle progression. Earlier desktop explorations are not part of the final deliverable.
